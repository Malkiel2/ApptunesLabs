Soap Studio privacy notice. AppTunes Labs / Malkiel Shaul provides Soap Studio. Contact: apptuneslabs@gmail.com. Updated 31 October 2026.

Request deletion of your data

To request deletion of purchase-service or support data associated with Soap Studio, email apptuneslabs@gmail.com with the subject “Soap Studio data deletion request”. Tell us whether your request concerns purchase-service data or a support message. We may ask for information needed to identify the relevant record; please do not send payment card details or passwords. We will respond to your request and explain any applicable retention.

Workshop records stay on your device; to remove them, delete the app and separately delete any exports, safety copies or device backups you no longer want. Deleting purchase-service data may affect purchase recognition or restoration. Apple or Google may retain transaction records for their own legal, fraud-prevention and accounting purposes under their respective policies.

Workshop data on your device

Recipes, ingredient records, purchases, batch history, prices, notes, workshop name and attached photos are stored in the app’s local storage. Soap Studio does not send this workshop content to AppTunes Labs or RevenueCat. No workshop account is required, and there is no automatic application-server sync. We do not run advertising, third-party advertising tracking or a general-purpose behavioral analytics SDK in the app.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple or Google processes payments. We do not receive payment-card details. Soap Studio uses RevenueCat to display its purchase screen, validate purchases, restore ownership and provide purchase-related reporting. The SDK can contact RevenueCat during app startup and purchase-related use, even if you do not buy Pro. RevenueCat processes a random app user identifier, receipts or purchase tokens, transaction and entitlement information, and technical context such as app/OS version, platform, locale and currency. Purchase-screen interactions may also be processed for purchase analytics. We do not supply your name, email address, recipes, photos or advertising identifier to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection is disabled in our SDK configuration. Network providers necessarily process connection information when delivering these services. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy for its processing and retention.

Files, photos and system services

You choose files through system pickers and choose where to save or share exports and backups. Other apps or cloud storage providers you select receive the files you share with them under their own policies. Your operating system may include app data in its device backups according to your settings. Notification permission is requested only when you enable local reminders. Reminders are scheduled on the device. Photo access uses the platform picker; we do not upload your photo library.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue, and keep them only as needed for support, dispute handling or legal requirements. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. We do not sell workshop data or support messages.

Retention and your choices

Local workshop data stays until you delete it, replace it through restore, or remove the app. Exported files, safety copies and operating-system backups must be managed separately in their storage locations. Purchase records are held by the platform store and RevenueCat to validate and restore ownership, prevent fraud, handle refunds and satisfy applicable retention obligations. Removing the app does not erase those purchase records. You can contact us about access, correction or deletion requests; we may need enough information to identify the relevant purchase or support record. Store transaction records and legally required records may need to be retained.

Security and international processing

The app uses platform storage protections and encrypted network connections for purchase services. You control exported backup files and should store them securely. RevenueCat, Apple, Google and your selected support or storage providers may process information in countries other than yours under their respective safeguards and privacy notices. No storage or transmission system is guaranteed to be risk-free.

Children and changes

Soap Studio is a workshop tool for soapmakers and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information. Contact us if you believe such information was provided. We may update this notice when the app or its processing changes; the date above identifies this version.
