A little help for your workshop.

Find a quick answer, or get in touch with AppTunes Labs about Soap Studio.

Start with a recipe

Choose Recipes → New recipe. Enter an oils weight, add oils and bring their percentages to 100%. Choose your process, superfat, NaOH purity and water method. Check your supplier’s ingredient information before making. Melt-and-pour recipes use a prepared base and do not calculate added NaOH.

Plan, weigh, then follow your batch

Open a saved recipe and plan a batch. The batch keeps the exact recipe version and ingredient data used at that time. In make mode, check off weighed materials and record what you actually used. Unmolding and curing dates are reminders to inspect your soap; they do not certify that it is ready or safe.

Free and lifetime Pro

Free includes unlimited recipes, calculation, batch history, make mode, manual cost estimates and portable backups. Pro adds connected material inventory and purchase lots, historical purchase costing, aggregate shopping needs, saved pricing, finished-stock movements and branded reports. The native store purchase screen shows the current price. Pro is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription.

Restore Pro on another device

Use the same Apple App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase, connect to the internet, then open Settings → Restore purchases. A workshop backup does not transfer purchase ownership. Apple purchases and Google purchases are separate; cross-store ownership transfer is not currently supported. A pending payment unlocks Pro only after the store confirms it.

Keep a copy of your work

Open Settings → Save a full backup and choose a location you control. Keep the file somewhere outside the phone before changing devices or uninstalling. Full backups include recipes, batches, purchases and attached photos. Restore validates the file before replacing local workshop data and keeps a safety copy. CSV/JSON import instead adds reviewed records without replacing existing recipes. Never import an unreviewed formula.

What works offline?

Recipes, calculation, batches, inventory, costing and local files work without an application server or account. Buying or restoring Pro and downloading the purchase screen require internet access. An already verified Pro purchase is cached by the purchase SDK. There is no automatic workshop sync between devices.

Reminders and language

Enable Workshop reminders in Settings and allow notifications when the system asks. Device notification settings, battery management and time-zone changes can affect delivery; reminders are approximate. The app starts in your device language and falls back to English. Choose another language or the device default in Settings → Language.

Contact support

Email apptuneslabs@gmail.com. Include Soap Studio, your device, OS version, app version and the steps that caused the problem. For a purchase issue, include the store and approximate purchase date. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or a full workshop backup unless it is needed and you choose to share it. There is no support chat or account sign-in inside the app.
